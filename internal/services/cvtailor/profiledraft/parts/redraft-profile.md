# Redraft the profile

Rewrite the paragraph in [profile.md](profile.md) so it speaks to the role in [job.md](job.md). Write it the way [voice.md](voice.md) describes.

## Make a minimal edit
- Keep the profile's structure and sentence order. Swap phrases; don't start again.
- Aim at how job.md says engineering works there, and at fit: backend or frontend lean, cross-functional work, product focus, AI-led development.
- Leave technologies to the bullets.

## Stay grounded
- Use only facts profile.md states. Never invent numbers, employers, scope or outcomes.
- Stay within the word budget in profile.md.

## Reply
The new paragraph only: one paragraph, no preamble, heading or quotes.
