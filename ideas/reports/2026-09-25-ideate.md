# Ideation Report

_Generated: 2026-09-25 · Codebase: job-scraper · Prior runs: 2026-07-01, 2026-06-24_

## Selected build target

**Morning Brief** — first slice: daily email of new roles ≥ notify_threshold and closed roles at tracked companies from the last 24h, backed by a `company_events` log (opened/closed/reopened) written at Board-poll time; recorded in `notification_digests`, skipped when empty.

---

## Codebase Characterisation

Same positioning as `ideas/CONTEXT.md` (multi-user, *selective and intelligent*, moat = jobs × CV
variants × per-user LLM rubric). **Delta since 2026-07-01:** every merged PR since has gone into
*acquisition* — a shared Company catalogue, Tracked Companies, verified multi-Board association,
complete ordered Board polls with Closed Job detection, retained Job Candidates, a durable
scoring outbox with per-user notification routing, RabbitMQ source queues. None of the nine
pending ideas shipped.

**The consequence:** the product now *observes* a lot — roles opening, closing, reopening on every
tracked Board — and *says* almost nothing about it. Observed gaps (all verified in code):

- Board polls set/clear `jobs.closed_at` (`queries/board_poll.sql:77-83`) but record no event;
  reopening is silent and the job DTO sent to the frontend has no `ClosedAt`.
- The only outbound signal is one instant email per first-discovered job ≥ `notify_threshold`
  (`internal/score/outbox.go:75-95`). `notification_digests` is unused and has no `user_id`.
- `companies_.$id.tsx` job table has no-op `onTrack`/`onEdit`; Check Frequency is accepted by the
  API (`tracked_companies.check_interval_minutes`) but has no control.
- `frontend/public/manifest.json` exists; no service worker.

## Interview Summary

- The user **hasn't used the app much yet** — infrastructure hasn't produced a daily habit.
- What would bring them back each morning: **news about the companies they track**.

## Market Landscape

_Carried forward from CONTEXT.md (Teal, Huntr, Simplify, career-ops, OSS trackers). This run adds
the company-watch niche._

**Page-diff monitors — Visualping, PageCrawl, UptimeRobot job alerts.** Paste a careers URL, get
an email/Slack/Telegram ping (Visualping attaches a screenshot of the diff) when the page changes.
Strong: zero setup, any site. Weak: a *diff*, not a *job* — no dedupe, no fit, no memory of what
this company did last time; every tracked company is equally loud.

**TrueUp.** Tracks openings at 9,000+ tech companies; hiring trends by category, "Hot 200"
fastest-growing ranking, big-tech hiring/layoff status. Strong: corpus-level momentum. Weak:
market-level, not *you*-level — momentum isn't intersected with your fit, your CVs, or your
application history.

**Hiring.cafe (+ Apify monitors).** Aggregates 250k+ live postings from career pages/ATSes; third
parties sell snapshot-diff "hiring change" actors on top. Reveals demand for ATS-level change
detection, sold as raw webhooks to people who then have to build the filtering themselves.

### Eureka moments

- **The poller is already a change-detection engine with no output.** The market sells change
  detection as the product (Visualping screenshots, Apify snapshot diffs). This repo already has
  the stronger primitive — complete, ordered ATS snapshots per verified Board (#178) with
  per-user scoring behind them — and throws the diff away. The whole "company news" product is
  *record the transitions you already compute, then filter by fit*.
- **Momentum × fit is an empty quadrant.** TrueUp has momentum without fit; Teal/Huntr/career-ops
  have fit without momentum. Owning both per user is only possible because Board polling and
  per-user scoring live in the same database.

## Premises

1. The missing piece is a **daily output surface**, not more acquisition — agreed ✓
2. Company news only matters **filtered through the user's fit**; reuse per-user scoring, not raw
   counts — agreed ✓
3. First channel is a **once-a-day email digest** replacing instant emails; in-app feed second —
   agreed ✓ (*with the note: instant alerts retain value as mobile push*)
4. One substrate — an append-only **company event log** written where polls and the scoring
   outbox already make transitions — feeds digest, feed, push and timelines — agreed ✓

## Desired Outcome

Every morning the user gets a short, fit-filtered account of what changed at the companies they
care about — and a buzz in their pocket only when something genuinely worth acting on lands.

## Opportunities → Solutions

### Opportunity A: The system sees company changes but records none of them

#### 1. Company Event Log + Timeline · _Tier: adjacent_

**Problem.** A role at a tracked company opens, closes after 9 days, reopens a fortnight later —
the user sees none of it. `closed_at` is overwritten in place; history is gone.

**Why this codebase is uniquely positioned.** Board polls already compute exact open/close
transitions against a complete snapshot (`board_poll.sql`, `board_job_observations`,
`last_snapshot_version`); `jobs.first_discovered_at` and `content_changed_at` already exist. This is
an `INSERT` beside each existing `UPDATE`, not a new detector.

**Architectural leverage.** New `company_events` table (company_id, job_id, board_id, kind ∈
opened/closed/reopened/content_changed, at). Written in the same transaction as the poll's
closed_at updates and first-discovery ingest. Rendered as a timeline card on
`companies_.$id.tsx`; `ClosedAt` added to the job DTO.

**Risks.** Aggregator-sourced jobs (no Board) produce fewer, noisier transitions — scope v1 to
Verified Boards only.

**Tracer-bullet first slice.** Record `opened`/`closed`/`reopened` from Board polls; show the last
30 days as a timeline on the company detail page.

#### 2. Tracked-company cockpit finish · _Tier: adjacent (finish what's 60% there)_

**Problem.** The company page is read-only in practice: no-op row actions, no Check Frequency
control, closed roles invisible.

**Why uniquely positioned.** Every backend piece exists (`check_interval_minutes`, job track/edit
flows on `jobs.tsx`, `GET /applications/for-jobs` with no caller). Pure wiring.

**Tracer-bullet first slice.** Wire track/edit on company job rows, add a Check Frequency select,
show open/closed toggle using `ClosedAt`.

### Opportunity B: No daily ritual — nothing arrives worth opening

#### 3. Morning Brief — fit-filtered daily digest · _Tier: expansion_ ⭐ recommended

**Problem.** The user doesn't open the app. The only outbound signal is per-job instant email,
which they say has little value.

**Existing gaps.** Visualping/PageCrawl ping per change with no fit; TrueUp sends market news
not tied to you.

**Why uniquely positioned.** Per-user scores, matched/missing chips and rationale already sit in
`job_scores`; Resend + React-email pipeline (`emails/`, `internal/notify`) is live; a
`notification_digests` table was already envisioned. The brief joins #1's events with
`job_scores` for the recipient — the *momentum × fit* quadrant no competitor occupies.

**Architectural leverage.** Re-shape `notification_digests` (add `user_id`, window bounds) as the
idempotency record; one scheduled job per user in `cmd/worker`; new `emails/templates/brief.tsx`.
Sections: *New roles that fit you* (≥ notify_threshold, grouped by tracked company) · *Closed*
(roles you'd scored or tracked) · *Reopened* · *Quiet companies* (no change — collapsed).
Instant email in `outbox.go` switches off by default (becomes push in #4).

**Risks.** Empty days — send nothing rather than an empty brief; decide send-time/timezone
(profile field needed).

**Tracer-bullet first slice.** Daily email listing *new roles at tracked companies scoring ≥
threshold in the last 24h* plus *closed roles you'd seen*, recorded in `notification_digests`,
skipped when empty. Depends on #1's `opened`/`closed` events.

#### 4. Pocket alerts — Web Push for the rare urgent hit · _Tier: expansion_

**Problem.** When a genuinely great role lands the user may be away from a desk; email is too
slow/noisy, the brief is a day late.

**Why uniquely positioned.** The trigger already exists and is exactly-once-ish:
`FirstDiscovery && score ≥ threshold` in the scoring outbox with a pluggable `sendAlert`. The
frontend already ships a PWA `manifest.json`. Swap the sink, add a second, higher threshold.

**Architectural leverage.** Service worker + VAPID Web Push (installed PWA push works on iOS 16.4+
and Android); `push_subscriptions` table; `sendAlert` becomes channel routing: push for ≥
`push_threshold` (e.g. 85) on tracked companies, everything else waits for the brief. New Go dep
for Web Push encryption (or ntfy.sh as a zero-frontend stopgap).

**Risks.** iOS requires "Add to Home Screen" before push is allowed; the settings page must walk
the user through it.

**Tracer-bullet first slice.** Settings toggle subscribes this browser; a job ≥ push threshold at a
tracked company pushes title + score + deep link to `jobs/$id`.

### Opportunity C: Company signal beyond "a role appeared"

#### 5. Hiring momentum & repost signals · _Tier: expansion_ · _complements pending "ghost-risk"_

**Problem.** "Acme has 12 roles" says nothing. "Acme's roles in your family close in a median 11
days; this one was reposted twice" tells you whether to hurry or be wary.

**Existing gaps.** TrueUp shows momentum but not per role family you'd score well in; nobody
flags reposts at ATS level.

**Why uniquely positioned.** With #1's event log: time-to-close, reopen counts and open-role
delta per company are SQL aggregates. Canonical job identity via trusted ATS posting IDs
(`job_urls`, idempotent batch ingest #157) makes "same role reposted under a new ID vs genuinely
new" answerable — page-diff tools can't. Distinct from the pending *ghost-risk* idea, which uses
the user's *application outcomes*; this uses the *company's board behaviour*. They combine later.

**Risks.** Needs weeks of event history before numbers mean anything — ship #1 early so the clock
starts.

**Tracer-bullet first slice.** On company detail: open-role count 30d trend, median days-to-close,
and a "reposted" badge on jobs whose title+board reappeared within 60 days.

#### 6. Companies like the ones you track · _Tier: expansion_

**Problem.** Tracking is manual; the user only hears about companies they already know.

**Why uniquely positioned.** The Company catalogue is **shared across users** and fed by
harvesters (YC/Getro, careers crawl). Jobs at untracked-but-verified companies can be ranked with
the free heuristic Relevance filters (no LLM spend — on-thesis), and co-tracking by other users is a
signal no single-user tool has.

**Risks.** Privacy of co-tracking signal in multi-user — aggregate only, thresholded.
`scope risk: medium` — pulls toward breadth.

**Tracer-bullet first slice.** A "Suggested" section in the brief: top 3 untracked verified
companies by count of open roles passing your heuristic filters, one-click Track.

### Opportunity D: Timing is invisible

#### 7. Apply-window forecast · _Tier: moonshot_

**Problem.** Fit tells you *whether*; nothing tells you *when*. Roles at some companies vanish in
a week, others sit for months (or are evergreen/ghost).

**Why uniquely positioned.** Once #1/#5 accumulate history across the shared catalogue and *all*
users' tracked Boards, each (company, role family) gets an empirical survival curve. Combined with
per-user fit, the brief can rank by *fit × urgency* — "apply today, this closes fast here". No
competitor has both ATS-level lifecycle history and per-user fit in one store.

**Risks.** Sparse data for small companies; needs a fallback prior per ATS/role family. Months of
history before it's credible. `scope risk: high` until #1 has run for a quarter.

**Tracer-bullet first slice.** "Typically open ~N days here" label on high-fit roles, computed
from closed events at that company, shown only when n ≥ 5.
